Renew software on evidence, not on autopilot.

SeatSignal brings your subscriptions, contracts, license assignments and imported usage reports into one place. It flags licenses that look underused and shows the data behind each flag, so IT and finance can decide what to keep before the notice deadline.

Evidence, not assumptions

Each recommendation lists the contract clause, roster record or usage export behind it. If no usage data has been imported, SeatSignal says utilization is unknown.

People make the decisions

SeatSignal proposes actions for review. Account changes and cancellations stay with the system owner and go through your approval workflow.

Honest savings math

Opportunities are shown as estimated ranges with the method spelled out. A saving counts as verified only after finance records the amended order or invoice.

Platform

Six capabilities, one renewal workflow

AI does the reading and the drafting. Seat counts, utilization and savings figures come from a deterministic analysis engine, so every number can be traced to a source record.

Subscription intelligence

Extracts product names, renewal dates, costs, seat counts, notice periods and price caps from order forms and agreements. Each field shows the page and passage it came from, plus a confidence level. Missing terms are flagged.

License utilization analysis

Compares your HRIS roster and identity-provider assignments with imported vendor activity reports. It finds inactive and unassigned seats and licenses still held by people who have left.

Renewal risk monitoring

Scheduled checks track renewal and notice dates. They flag auto-renewals whose notice window has closed, contracts missing from the file, and terms the extractor could not find.

Spend anomaly detection

Uses your recorded invoice history to spot unit-price increases and seat growth that outpaces headcount, and links each finding to the invoices behind it.

Consolidation recommendations

Combines overlapping functions, low observed usage and renewal timing to rank the subscriptions most worth investigating. Each one lists its data gaps.

Executive reporting

Produces board-ready summaries of spend, utilization evidence, renewal exposure and proposed actions. Estimated and verified figures are never mixed.

How it works

From scattered records to an approved renewal plan

  1. 1

    Consolidate

    Upload contracts and invoices, and import your HRIS roster and identity-provider app assignments.

  2. 2

    Import usage

    Add vendor admin activity exports, or connect an authorized integration. Subscriptions without usage data stay marked "unknown".

  3. 3

    Review evidence

    Go through recommendations ranked by deadline. Each shows its sources, confidence and data gaps.

  4. 4

    Approve & verify

    Owners approve or reject. The system owner makes the change, and finance records the verified saving.

Use cases

Built for the people who own the renewal

Know who holds what, and whether they use it

  • Map every license to a person through your identity-provider export and HRIS roster.
  • Find licenses still assigned to people who have left, for your offboarding process.
  • Import vendor activity reports and see the activity window and data gaps behind each figure.

Forecast renewals and track savings you can defend

  • Use the renewal calendar to see exposure by month, with auto-renew and notice terms.
  • Catch unit-price increases and seat growth that outpaces headcount, linked to the invoices.
  • Keep a savings ledger that separates estimated, approved and verified amounts.
  • Generate executive summaries that state their data coverage.

Trust & architecture

Designed to inform decisions, not make them

  • No automatic account changes. SeatSignal has no permission to disable users, remove licenses or cancel subscriptions.
  • No inferred usage. Without an imported report or an authorized integration, a subscription's utilization is shown as unknown.
  • Every number is traceable. The AI writes narrative around figures computed from your records. It does not invent them.
  • Read-only integrations. Connectors request the narrowest read scopes the vendor offers.
